# Topic

## Research question
Does high school math achievement causally increase the probability of choosing a STEM major?

## Working causal framing
- **Treatment / exposure proxy:** high school math achievement or math-related preparedness
- **Outcome:** intended STEM major or declared STEM major in postsecondary study
- **Target estimand:** effect of higher math achievement on the probability of choosing STEM

## Likely confounders
- family background
- demographic characteristics
- prior STEM interest
- school preparation
- self-efficacy / enjoyment in math

## Current dataset caveat
The current NCES files in `data/sources/` appear to be summary tables rather than student-level microdata. That may limit us to descriptive or associational evidence unless richer unit-level data is added.
