# Research Note 02: Causal Findings - The Calculus Dividend

**Focus:** Primary Treatment Effects & Model Triangulation  
**Treatment:** Calculus Completion in High School  
**Outcome:** Declaring a STEM Major (4-year Postsecondary)

## 1. Executive Summary
The completion of Calculus serves as a powerful "Gatekeeper" to STEM pathways. Using Augmented Inverse Probability Weighting (AIPW), we estimate a robust **Average Treatment Effect (ATE) of 36.6%**. This suggests that Calculus is the single most significant predictor in our model, even after adjusting for demographics and psychosocial factors.

## 2. Model Triangulation
To ensure the robustness of the 36.6% estimate, we employed five different causal estimators. The consistency across models provides high confidence in the result.

**Key Insight:** The consensus around ~36% indicates that the "Calculus Dividend" is a persistent feature of the data, regardless of the functional form of the estimator.

## 3. The "Package" Effect
By comparing the raw Calculus coefficient (OLS) with the AIPW estimate:
- **Raw Correlation:** Significant association.
- **Adjusted ATE (36.6%):** Shows that the effect remains dominant even when comparing students with identical SES, gender, and academic ambitions.
